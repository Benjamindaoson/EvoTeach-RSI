# Honest CV and Faculty-Outreach Positioning

## Current project status

**EvoTeach-RSI — Independent Research Prototype (2026–present)**

GitHub: https://github.com/Benjamindaoson/EvoTeach-RSI

- Designed a research framework for evidence-gated improvement of educational agents, explicitly distinguishing adaptive tutoring from revisions to the **teaching update mechanism**.
- Implemented a reproducible synthetic reference with bounded learner-state update rules, versioned meta-search steps, separate validation/test cohorts and regression safeguards.
- Developed an experimental protocol centered on independent transfer, delayed retention, calibration and equal-budget baselines.

Do **not** claim measured improvements in real students, peer-reviewed publication, production deployment, or supervision by a particular professor. Such outcomes are not established by the v0.1 prototype.

## Short research introduction

"I am building EvoTeach-RSI, an independent research prototype examining whether AI tutors can diagnose failures in the procedures they use to learn from teaching feedback, then safely propose and evaluate revisions to those procedures. I would like to test the mechanism on independent transfer and retention tasks with strong fixed-adaptive baselines and a clearly bounded research scope."
